Denver Group Rides

Every group ride you're looking for, all in one place! This site pulls upcoming rides from cycling clubs and shops across metro Denver, Boulder, Fort Collins and Colorado's mountain towns, and displays them on an interactive map. Boulder rides come from boulderrides.cc.

Free, open source, and modeled on Boulder Group Rides by Roger Bergua, whose MIT-licensed frontend this site is built from. If you find this useful, please support the original project.

Why this exists

Boulder has had one map for all of its group rides since March 2026. Denver did not. Finding out what's happening, when, and where meant checking a dozen club pages. This project puts it all in one place.

A backend process pulls the latest events several times a day from Ride with GPS club pages, Meetup group calendars, the Bicycle Colorado calendar and (once connected) Strava clubs. Boulder rides are merged in from the boulderrides.cc public data feed. It requires zero effort from the clubs themselves.

Every ride is checked before it is shown: the distance is re-measured from the route itself, the start point must be in Colorado, and a ride posted on two platforms is listed once. Phone numbers and email addresses are removed from descriptions.

Clubs on the map

The following clubs are checked on every update. To suggest a new one, open an issue on GitHub.

Blue-labeled sources are pulled directly on every update. Green-labeled clubs are Boulder clubs, carried over as published by boulderrides.cc. Gray-labeled clubs post their rides on Strava and appear on the map once Strava API access is connected. Rides anywhere in Colorado are shown; the area chips group them by where they start.

Weather forecast

Forecasts are provided by the National Weather Service API for downtown Denver and updated with every data refresh.

Displayed forecast times:

Summer (Daylight Saving Time): 9 AM & 4 PM
Winter (Standard Time): 10 AM & 2 PM

Detailed weather along the route is available for some rides when the average speed is known (for Denver rides, when the organizer states a single pace in the event description). You can also explore weather along your own ride at rideaheadweather.com.

How to use it

Pick a day on the calendar. Highlighted days have rides or races/events, and the first available one loads automatically.

Click or tap a route or its pin to see details and available weather. On desktop, hover for a quick preview. Close the card or select another ride to keep exploring.

Solid lines are paved, dashed lines are unpaved (e.g., gravel). Each route has a pin that marks its start.

Checkered-flag pins mark upcoming races near Denver and Boulder. Tap the Races button to browse them, or click any checkered pin on the map to open the Races page with that event highlighted.

Use the area picker at the bottom to show Denver, Boulder, Fort Collins or mountain rides only, and List to see the whole day as a list. A ride's area is decided by where it starts.

Share on a ride card copies a link that opens this map straight on that ride. Directions opens the meeting point in Google Maps, and Add to calendar saves the ride to Apple Calendar, Outlook or Google Calendar. The button at the bottom of the card opens the organizer's own event page, which is where you join or RSVP; when the organizer posted a registration or waiver link, a green button for it appears above.

Rides whose organizer posted no route appear as a start pin only and are marked No route posted.

Use the Metric button to display the ride statistics in km/meters and the temperature in °C.

Mobile app

There is no need to download a separate app. This site adapts automatically to phones and tablets. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

On Android: open in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right, and select "Install and create shortcut".

On iPhone: open in Safari, tap the share button (↑) at the bottom, and select "Add to Home Screen".

Races & Events

Upcoming races and events for the next 2 months across the same area as the rides: the Front Range from Denver to Fort Collins and Colorado's mountain towns. Whether you're racing, spectating, or volunteering, you'll find them here. Data is pulled from the BikeReg API with every data refresh.

Major events that are not listed on bikereg.com can be added by hand in the backend (backend/extra_events.json).
